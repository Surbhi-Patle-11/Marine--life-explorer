what is marine life ?

Marine life, also known as sea or ocean life, encompasses all plants, animals, and other organisms that inhabit the Earth's saltwater environments. It represents a significant portion of the world's biodiversity, with scientists estimating that up to two million species may exist, though less than 250,000 have been formally documented.

key features

Habitat:Primarily saltwater, distinguishing it from freshwater life.

Diversity:Encompasses all kingdoms of life: animals, plants, protists, fungi, and bacteria.

Size Range:From microscopic phytoplankton to the massive blue whale, the largest animal on Earth.
